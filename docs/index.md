features:
  - title: One scale slider
    details: 1x to 3x in 0.25 steps per display. Uses the monitor's own modes, or a virtual HiDPI display for the sizes macOS won't give you.
  - title: Brightness and volume
    details: Over DDC/CI, in the same 16 steps as the built-in keys. Your keyboard keys work again, with the familiar on-screen popup.
  - title: Remembers each monitor
    details: Unplug, plug back in, and your scale comes back. A quick cable wiggle doesn't flash the screen.
  - title: Says why it can't
    details: If your Mac, port or monitor can't do something, the menu and Settings tell you, instead of silently doing nothing.
  - title: Small and native
    details: Swift, no Electron, no dependencies, no account, no tracking.
  - title: Free and open source
    details: MIT licensed. Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
